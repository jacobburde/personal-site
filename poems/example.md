---
title: First Light
texture: dark-paper
border: vine
order: 1
---

Before the words were carved,
before the plate was scored,
there was only the waiting dark
and the tool's bright edge.

Now light emerges
line by patient line,
each letter a small wound
through which meaning shines.

The page becomes a window,
the ink a door ajar,
and what was solid darkness
opens into stars.
